What makes it different

Generic templates get ignored.
Personalized messages get reviews.

A message that says "Mike — we appreciated the trust you put in us for the roof at Clearwater" lands completely differently than "Hi [Name], we hope you loved your recent service." The AI writes the former, automatically, for every job.

  • Customer name from your check-in dataNo copy-paste, no fill-in-the-blank. The AI uses the name you captured on the job.
  • Job address in the messageReferences the actual location — the detail homeowners remember most.
  • Sends from your own phone or emailNo third-party number. No platform fee per text. The message comes from you.
  • Your review link included automaticallyThe Google review link from your Account settings is pre-populated in every draft.
  • Your AI agent handles every replyWarranty questions, job confirmation, follow-up details — answered using your actual job record and business profile, not generic scripts.
  • Knows when to involve youIf a customer is unhappy, your AI agent pauses, stops responding, and notifies you immediately. It knows when a human needs to take over.
