How it works

Four steps. Zero extra effort.

Your AI copywriting agent works alongside your crew without them changing a thing. They submit photos. Your agent handles everything that comes next.

1
Crew checks in with photos

Address, trade, and a few job photos. Notes are optional — the AI doesn't need them to write a good description.

2
Your AI agent reads every photo

Identifies what was done — materials, scope, before and after state — directly from the images. No notes needed from your crew.

3
Draft appears on your dashboard

You review the generated description. Edit anything you want, or publish as-is. It takes 30 seconds.

4
Job page goes live

A location-specific, indexed page — tied to the job address — goes live and starts building your local search presence.

What it writes

Not a template. A real description of your real job.

Your AI copywriting agent writes from what's actually in the photos — the trade, the materials, the condition before and after. Think of it as a professional writing employee who was on the job site with your crew, taking notes you'll never have to write yourself.

  • Location-specific from the first sentenceCity, street address, and neighborhood baked in — the way Google's local search algorithm expects it.
  • Materials and scope from the photosReads what was actually used and what was done — not generic filler copy about the trade.
  • Professional tone, contractor voiceWritten to sound like an experienced contractor wrote it — not like marketing copy or AI boilerplate.
  • Editable before it publishesYou always review before anything goes live. Change a word or the whole thing — it's your page.
  • Schema, alt text, and internal links at every publishStructured data, image captions from photo analysis, and links to related jobs in the same city and trade — added automatically. Nothing to configure.
